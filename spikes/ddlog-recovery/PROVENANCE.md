(BFS and finite-set oracle) are adapted from `everettVT/ddlog-runtime`, commit
`3c377e6915bb640b2e6cb3ebf8c7fa0433ecc5c4`, respectively

to this repository and uses its root MIT license.

The prototype originated as a local September 5, 2026 acceptance. Publishing it
does not add an Archetype kernel or standalone runtime persistence backend.
